Philosophy

How I think about creative work.

Design is not decoration. Storytelling is not content. Leadership is not management.

Emotion before aesthetics

Not "does it look good?" but "what does it make you feel?" Emotional response first — everything else follows.

Curiosity as method

Better questions beat more answers. I bring genuine curiosity to every brief — and protect it in the teams I lead.

Intention over instinct

Gut feel is a starting point. Every decision should withstand a clear "why" — from concept to final pixel.

Scale without dilution

Making something that holds its quality at global scale — that's the hardest problem. I've spent 20 years on it.
