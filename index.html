About

I'm Daniel — a creative leader who has spent over two decades working at the intersection of brand, technology, and human experience. I've built campaigns that reached billions, led teams across continents, and earned ~100 industry awards along the way. But what drives me isn't recognition. It's resonance.

I started in Stockholm, sharpening my craft in small studios before joining North Kingdom, where I helped build one of the world's most awarded digital creative agencies. Later, Meta brought me to California to shape brand and product experience at a scale few ever encounter.

I'm a patent holder, an Emmy nominee, and — most importantly — someone who genuinely loves the work. The difficult questions. The unexpected answers. The moment a brief becomes something that outlasts the campaign.

Philosophy

How I think about creative work.

Design is not decoration. Storytelling is not content. Leadership is not management. These distinctions matter — and they shape every decision I make.

Emotion before aesthetics

The question is never "does it look good?" It's "what does it make you feel?" Great design earns an emotional response first — everything else follows.

Curiosity as method

The best ideas come from asking better questions, not from having more answers. I bring genuine curiosity to every brief — and protect that curiosity in the teams I lead.

Intention over instinct

Gut feel is a starting point, not a destination. Every creative decision should be able to withstand a clear "why" — from concept to final pixel.

Scale without dilution

The hardest creative challenge is making something that holds its quality at global scale. I've spent 20 years solving exactly that problem.
